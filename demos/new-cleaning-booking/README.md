# New Cleaning Booking Demo

## Purpose
Isolated lead-magnet preview of a fictional cleaning company. Visitors experience the booking flow before seeing The Launch Era offer at the bottom. Not production.

## Preview source
Open `index.html` locally in a browser. Public preview deployment pending.

## Booking flow
1. Choose Standard, Deep, Move-In/Out, or Commercial Cleaning and frequency.
2. Provide property type, size, bedrooms, bathrooms and full address.
3. Review and enter contact information.

The completion is explicitly a simulation: no live booking, email, payment, or customer record is created.
