Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026

CB Depot operates a customer inquiry and booking page for car detailing services. This page explains how information you choose to share through that service is handled.

Information you submit

When you request a quote, appointment or response, the form may collect your name, contact information, vehicle type and condition, selected services, preferred appointment date or time, address if provided, and details you write in your message.

How that information is used

The information is used to review requests, prepare quotes, discuss availability, confirm appointments when approved, communicate about services and maintain business records. Sending a request does not automatically guarantee a confirmed appointment.

Service providers

CB Depot uses operational systems provided by The Launch Era and supporting hosting, database and email services to process booking-related information. These providers may process information to help deliver the requested functionality. Information is not intended for unrelated promotional use without the applicable permission.

Communication preferences

You may receive messages about a request, estimate or appointment. Optional follow-up communications depend on the business's configured settings and applicable permissions. You can reply to a message or use the contact form to ask for follow-ups to stop.

Storage and security

Business records are held in access-controlled systems. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Information is retained for business and legal needs and then deleted or anonymized when no longer required, subject to applicable retention obligations.

Requests about your information

You may ask CB Depot about correcting or deleting your information, or about its use, by contacting the business. Some records may need to be retained where required by law or to resolve transactions.

Updates

This policy may be revised if the booking system or the business's data practices change. The effective date above identifies this version.

Questions about these terms or your information? Contact CB Depot using the contact form on its Booking Page.
