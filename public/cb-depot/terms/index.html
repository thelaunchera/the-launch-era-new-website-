Booking Terms

Effective October 10, 2026

These terms describe inquiries and appointment requests placed through CB Depot's online detailing form. Review the details before submitting a request.

Requests are not confirmations

Submitting a form requests a service or quote; it does not guarantee a date, service slot or agreed price. Unless an appointment is explicitly confirmed, it remains pending CB Depot's review and availability.

Quotes and service details

Quotes may depend on vehicle type, size, condition, requested services and extras. CB Depot will confirm the scope, price and timing directly with the customer before the work is performed. An estimate on this page is not necessarily a final quote.

Changes and cancellations

If you need to change your request or cancel a confirmed appointment, reply to the confirmation or use the contact form as soon as possible. CB Depot will confirm any available options and any applicable cancellation terms before an additional charge is accepted.

Payments

The online request form does not itself collect payment. Any payment arrangements are confirmed directly with CB Depot. Do not send card numbers or other sensitive payment credentials through the free-text request form.

Accuracy

Please provide reasonably accurate contact, vehicle and scheduling details. Requests with incomplete details may require clarification before a quote or appointment is confirmed.

Service questions

Availability, service methods and any warranties or service guarantees, if offered, should be confirmed directly with CB Depot before booking.

Changes to these terms

CB Depot may update its booking policies. The terms visible when you submit a request help describe that request; separately agreed service terms may also apply.

Questions about these terms or your information? Contact CB Depot using the contact form on its Booking Page.
