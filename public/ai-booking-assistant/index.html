A few things worth knowing

What happens after you buy?

Can I use it without buying the Booking Automation + Command Center?

Yes. AI Booking Assistant is sold separately and can be installed on a compatible existing website. You do not need to buy our Booking Page to ask for AI setup.

Does the assistant confirm bookings or send emails for me?

The standalone assistant can answer approved questions and guide visitors toward your booking form. Real booking confirmations, calendar actions and automated emails require a separate verified integration. The demo does not perform those actions.

What am I charged today?

Pay only the one-time setup today ($79, $59, or $119 depending on your selected agent). Your first 30 days of monthly service are included. After that, the chosen monthly plan starts.

Can I put it on both my Booking Page and existing website?

Tell us both locations in the setup form. We'll confirm compatibility and placement before completing installation.

What information do you need?

After your payment is confirmed, you'll fill in a short setup form with your business name, website or Booking Page, services, and style preferences.
