Cookies and browser storage

Updated: October 26, 2026

Required storage

Local storage, session storage and account technologies support sign-in, secure access, language and settings, your privacy choice and App operation. Rejecting statistics does not erase your sign-in or business records.

Optional statistics

Google Analytics uses cookies such as _ga and _ga_* to measure use. We do not load its tag until you allow statistics. App visit statistics and assistant interaction metrics respect the same choice. Acceptance is not required to book or purchase.

Change your choice

Your choice is stored in this browser for 180 days. You can change it anytime; each domain and device has its own choice. Rejecting stops future statistics and removes Google Analytics cookies accessible from this domain. It does not automatically erase data already sent.

External services

Google Fonts, remotely hosted photos and payment or AI services may receive technical connection data when used. External platforms have their own policies. Statistics consent does not subscribe you to email.
