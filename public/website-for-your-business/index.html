Frequently Asked Questions

The details you need before getting started.

Can you work with any industry?

Yes. Websites and automations can be adapted to businesses across industries.

Where do $15 package notifications go?

Contact, quote or booking requests can be sent to the email inbox you choose.

How much is the monthly service, and what does it cover?

The AI + CRM package is $25 for setup plus $8 per month. The first charge is $33 (setup and first month); subsequent charges are $8 per month. Monthly service covers agreed updates to existing website photos, text, prices, offers and pop-up messages, not unlimited changes, new pages or unlimited third-party AI usage.

Does the $25 package include unlimited AI?

No. The $25 is the initial setup, plus an $8 monthly service. Any additional hosting, domain or third-party AI consumption costs are disclosed before work begins.

Does a booking request confirm an appointment?

Not by itself. An appointment is confirmed only if the approved workflow verifies availability and records it.
