Questions before you decide?

Here are the details that matter before you begin.

Can you build a digital menu or service website?

Yes. Choose a digital menu, product catalog or services website with inquiry or reservation requests. Initial products and prices are published during setup.

Will my business and customers receive emails?

Yes. New inquiries are sent to the inbox you choose, and customers receive an automatic email acknowledging their request. Your team handles any next step that needs a personal response.

What does the Package 2 monthly service cover?

Package 2 costs $25 for setup, then $8/month for the advanced CRM and email service after the first month, counted from delivery. An AI agent is not included; you can request it separately, with pricing confirmed before activation.

How are restaurant tables confirmed?

Guests choose their date and time. With availability checking connected, an available table is confirmed by email. Every inquiry also receives an automatic email acknowledgment, while your team personally handles requests that need follow-up.

Is Command Center included with the $15 website?

Yes. Your private Command Center helps you manage inquiries, customers and catalog updates. Advanced automation is in Package 2. An AI agent is a separate optional request.

Can you improve a website built on another platform?

Yes, after reviewing the URL, platform and requested changes. We can work within the capabilities of WordPress, Wix, Shopify, Squarespace, Webflow, Framer or code-based sites with access. If a direct edit is not practical, we will explain the option to create a new version before work begins.
