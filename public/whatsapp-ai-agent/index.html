Good to know

Is WhatsApp AI available?

Will it confirm every booking automatically?

No. Availability and business settings decide that. Custom estimates and special jobs stay pending until the owner reviews them.

Is this already included with an AI Assistant?

COMING SOON. WhatsApp AI is a separate planned channel. Explore the preview while we prepare for launch.

It’s a separate channel in preparation: $48 promotional setup through November ($78 afterward), plus proposed $14.99/month after the first 30 days from delivery. Checkout opens only after the integration is working and fully tested.

Do I need WhatsApp Business already?

No. The WhatsApp Business app is free, and we can help you set it up if needed. The automation requires a compatible WhatsApp Business Platform account and number. If you want to keep using an existing Business app number, we’ll check if Meta’s coexistence option is available.

Are there extra WhatsApp charges?

Meta may charge for delivered messages through its Business Platform. Your WhatsApp Business app itself is free. The business owns and pays its Meta usage costs directly, in addition to our monthly maintenance; we will explain the current rates before activation.

What permissions will you need?

An authorized Meta business administrator must connect the WhatsApp Business account and approve the messaging and management permissions. You’ll verify ownership of your number through Meta. We never ask for your Meta password or verification codes.
