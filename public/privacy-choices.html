Request access, correction or deletion

Write from the email associated with your account and tell us what you are requesting. We may ask for the minimum verification needed to avoid changing someone else’s data. Do not include identity documents, passwords or your clients’ data in your first message.

Request a review of my data

This opens our contact form. The request is sent only when you send the message. Deletion requires review; some records may need to be retained for legal, transaction or security purposes.

Optional emails

You can reply to a marketing email asking to opt out, or use our contact form: hello@thelaunchera.com. No sign-in or payment is needed. Necessary purchase or account messages are handled separately.

A cleaning business’s customer data

If a business using our tools entered your information, contact that business first. We can help review the request without disclosing or deleting another business’s records.
