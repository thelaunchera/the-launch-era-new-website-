1. What this service does

Self Love Gym Coach Desk helps independent coaches organize clients, sessions, memberships, manual payment records, notes, and reminders. Preview data is fictional and stays in your browser. Signed-in workspace data is saved to your private account.

2. Your responsibilities

You are responsible for the accuracy of your records, obtaining permission to store client contact information and send messages, and complying with laws that apply to your business. Only enter information you are authorized to use. Keep your account secure.

3. Payments and messages

Online membership checkout uses a Stripe Payment Link created in the coach’s own Stripe account. Stripe handles charges, subscription management and bank payouts under its own terms. Coach Desk stores the link but does not confirm online payment status, charge cards, cancel subscriptions or move funds. Cash and check entries are manual records. Reminders are a list for the coach; email and SMS buttons open a device composer and require the coach to review and send the message.

4. Fitness information

This is an administrative tool. It does not provide medical advice, assess a client’s health or replace professional judgment.

5. Availability and changes

We may update the service and these terms. Review important records independently and export data you need to retain. To ask about your account or these terms, contact hello@thelaunchera.com.

3. Pagos y mensajes

El pago de membresías en línea usa un enlace creado en la cuenta de Stripe de cada entrenadora. Stripe gestiona los cobros, las suscripciones y los depósitos bancarios bajo sus propios términos. Coach Desk guarda el enlace, pero no confirma pagos en línea, cobra tarjetas, cancela suscripciones ni mueve fondos. Los pagos en efectivo y cheque son registros manuales. Los recordatorios son una lista para la entrenadora; los botones de correo y SMS abren el editor del dispositivo y requieren que ella revise y envíe el mensaje.
