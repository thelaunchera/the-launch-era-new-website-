The spirit of Havana.

Havana 19 is an imagined tribute to the Cuban table: the aroma of slow-cooked dishes, conversations that stretch late into the night, and the warmth of being welcomed like family.

Here, familiar recipes meet a colorful, intimate atmosphere. Tropical blues, warm orange tones, candlelight and thoughtful hospitality make every meal feel like an occasion.

From Cuba, with soul. For Boston, with love.
