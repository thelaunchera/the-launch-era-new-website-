06 / GOOD TO KNOWYOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED
A few
sweet details.
Can I order a wedding cake directly online?+
No. Every cake begins with an inquiry. The bakery contacts you to discuss design, availability, pricing and next steps.
Are prices listed on the website?+
Yes, example prices appear alongside selected designs. The final personalized quote depends on the size, complexity, flavors, delivery and your event details.
Can I request a specific design?+
Absolutely. Share your ideas, favorite flowers or inspiration in the form, and the bakery can discuss what is possible.
Does submitting an inquiry reserve my date?+
Not yet. Your date is only confirmed after the bakery responds and both parties agree to the final order arrangements.
