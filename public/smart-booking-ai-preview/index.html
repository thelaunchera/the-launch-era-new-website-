GOOD QUESTIONS, CLEAR ANSWERS

A few things you may be wondering.

If you have a question about your particular business, use the offer link to start a conversation.

What is included?

The bundle is designed around a personalized landing and booking page, a three-step booking experience, a private Command Center, an optional customer-facing AI assistant, and supported follow-ups. Exact integrations are confirmed for your setup.

Can I use my own business name and photos?

Yes. The finished setup is intended to reflect your business name, services, and approved photos. This preview uses sample Launch Era imagery and interface examples.

Can customers book from their phones?

The booking experience is designed to work on mobile. Actual booking confirmation depends on the availability and connection configured for your business; this demonstration does not reserve a time.

Is the AI assistant optional?

Yes. It is optional and has a separate ongoing monthly subscription. The setup discount does not apply to monthly fees. This page's assistant is only an interactive preview.

Do I need technical experience?

No technical background is assumed. The setup is reviewed with you, and the final connection details are discussed before launch.

Are there monthly fees?

The AI assistant has a separate monthly subscription if you choose it. Other service or platform costs depend on the final setup and are confirmed before you decide; this preview does not configure prices.

Can the system be adapted to my industry?

The sample adapts across cleaning, detailing, landscaping, pressure washing, home services, and beauty. Your real services and questions are tailored to your business.

How does the setup process work?

You share information about your business, the system is customized, and you review the setup before launch. No delivery timeline is promised here.
