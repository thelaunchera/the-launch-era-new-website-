Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026.

The Launch Era provides websites, automation, lead management, optional AI assistants, and other agreed digital services for businesses across industries. This policy explains the information we may collect when you use our website, inquiry forms, client projects, demos, or existing services.

New websites, existing website updates, and optional AI agents

If you request an update to an existing website, we may collect its URL, platform, project goals and the access details you choose to share through an authorized channel. Please do not enter passwords, payment details or private customer records in public forms. Requesting information about an optional AI agent does not authorize its activation or a charge. We will agree on any separate data-processing scope before using an agent for your business.

Information we collect

Depending on what you use, we may collect contact details, business information, service and booking details, client records you choose to enter, account and authentication information, usage data, support messages, and billing or transaction status needed to provide a purchased service.

How we use it

We use information to provide and improve the service, create and maintain your account or project, process requests, send operational emails, provide support, prevent abuse, and keep the system secure.

Service providers

We may use trusted service providers for hosting, database services, email delivery, automation, analytics, file storage, and billing. They receive information only as needed to provide those functions.

Your business customers

If you use a website inquiry form, CRM, booking flow, or another business tool, you are responsible for the customer information you enter or collect for your business. The Launch Era uses that information to provide the tools and workflows you requested.

Checkout and order communications: Payments for our website packages are processed through Stripe. Connected automation providers, including Make, may create or verify checkout sessions; email providers may deliver order and onboarding messages. We do not request full card numbers through project-intake forms. Purchase and delivery messages are operational and do not mean consent to marketing.

Retention and security

We keep information for as long as reasonably needed to operate the service, meet legitimate business or legal needs, and maintain records. We use technical and organizational safeguards, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact us to ask about your information or request a correction or deletion where applicable. Some records may need to be retained for legal, security, or transaction purposes.

Contact The Launch Era

Who provides the service

The Launch Era is the brand providing these digital services for businesses across industries. Contact: hello@thelaunchera.com.

Analytics, imagery and supporting services

When you allow optional analytics, we may measure general use of the site and digital experiences to help improve them. You can change your choice in Cookie settings. Providing our services may involve hosting, account, messaging, project-management, AI and payment providers. We share only the information needed for the requested function, subject to applicable provider terms and privacy notices.

If you ask us to prepare or enhance images, we may process material you provide and use image-processing services to create the requested result. Do not send images or information you are not authorized to share. Optional AI chat messages may be processed to generate a response; please avoid passwords, payment-card information and sensitive details.

Payments your business collects from its customers are separate from services purchased from The Launch Era. See our cookie policy and manage your preferences through Cookie settings.

Children and data requests

These tools are intended for adult business operators. They are not designed for children under 13, and we do not knowingly request their personal information. Contact us if you believe a child’s information has been submitted so we can review it.

You can request access, correction or deletion. Requests are reviewed before action; submitting one does not automatically delete an account or another business’s records.
