Website & Automation Service Terms

Updated November 5, 2026

These terms govern our website and business automation packages for businesses across industries. We confirm each project's agreed scope before setup begins.

Our two website packages

Website for Your Business is $15 USD for an agreed one-time initial setup. It includes a responsive website, a contact/quote/appointment-request pathway, and notifications sent to the inbox chosen by the business; CRM is not included. Website + AI Assistant + Automated CRM is $25 USD for initial setup plus $8 USD per month, and adds an on-page AI assistant, an agreed lead-management CRM and an initial follow-up workflow. No separate purchase of our internal production workspace is necessary.

First charge and monthly billing

For the AI + CRM package, the first payment totals $33 USD: $25 initial setup plus the first $8 month. Thereafter, $8 USD is billed automatically every month until canceled. Stripe Checkout must display the charges before the customer authorizes payment. The $15 website package does not include this $8 monthly subscription.

What the $8 monthly service includes

While the subscription is active, you can request edits to photos, lettering and website copy, prices, offers, and pop-up announcements on the existing website. The service also supports upkeep of the agreed AI/CRM configuration. Requests are reviewed for complexity and feasibility: the fee is not an unlimited design or development subscription and does not include new websites, full redesigns, unlimited additional pages, new integrations, unlimited content creation, or unlimited third-party AI usage. Work beyond the approved scope is quoted separately and requires approval.

Renewals, cancellation and continuity

The monthly service renews automatically until canceled. You may request cancellation through our contact form before the next renewal date. We confirm the cancellation and its effect on ongoing edits, AI features and integrations that depend on external providers. Processing cancellation stops future recurring charges but does not cancel obligations already incurred. Continued site availability can depend on hosting, domain licenses and any independently paid third-party services. Applicable refund and consumer rights remain in force.

Production and delivery

Work starts once we confirm payment, receive the completed intake and agree on necessary assets and access. We organize production in a private internal workspace. Clients receive the finished website link and, where included in the $25 package, their own scoped access to the delivered CRM—not access to our internal administration. Delivery milestones are agreed at project kickoff and depend on timely provision of materials.

Requests, bookings, automations and AI

Submitting a booking or quote request does not alone confirm an appointment. A booking is confirmed only after an authorized workflow verifies availability and successfully records it. AI assistants can make mistakes and must be configured with authorized information; we do not guarantee error-free responses or sensitive autonomous decisions. Automated follow-ups require an appropriate legal basis, consent where required, and compliance with applicable messaging and privacy rules.

Domains, hosting and third-party expenses

The stated setup prices do not automatically include free domain registration, hosting, unlimited AI usage, transactional messaging or paid third-party integrations. Any required provider fees, renewals or usage limits are disclosed for approval before they are incurred. Customers are responsible for their own external accounts and licenses where applicable.

Content, data privacy and authorized access

Customers must supply content they are entitled to use, accurate business prices and policies, and permission to connect services. Do not submit passwords, payment card numbers or security codes in website intake forms. We process submitted contact and project information under our privacy policy. Customers remain responsible for legally contacting their own leads and customers.

Payments, revisions and previous agreements

Stripe processes payments. We do not guarantee leads, sales, bookings, revenue or search ranking outcomes. We coordinate agreed revisions during delivery; extra changes can require separate written approval. Previously accepted orders remain governed by their originally agreed terms rather than being changed retroactively.

Support and refunds

See our refund policy, privacy policy or contact us for questions about purchases, recurring billing, access, website changes or cancellation.
