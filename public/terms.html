Service terms

Last updated: November 5, 2026.

Current packages

We offer two starter packages to businesses across industries: Website for Your Business ($15 USD, one-time initial setup) and Website + AI Assistant + Automated CRM ($25 USD, one-time initial setup). Each project covers one agreed website and initial workflow, not unlimited development or permanent managed service.

Scope and approval

The $15 package includes a responsive business website, a contact/quote/booking-request path, and notifications delivered to the email address the business selects. The $25 package adds an on-page AI assistant, organized leads in an automated CRM, and an agreed follow-up workflow. We confirm the fields, integrations, assets and final scope before work begins. Automated follow-ups require an appropriate lawful basis or consent.

Pricing and third-party costs

The stated prices cover the agreed initial setup. Domain, hosting, website platform, AI provider, messages, upgrades and other third-party services may incur costs or limits. We disclose any necessary additional charges for approval before setup. Neither package promises unlimited AI usage or free hosting forever.

Requests and appointments

Submitting a request does not automatically confirm an appointment. Dates and times may only be confirmed when an approved workflow checks real availability and successfully records a booking. The customer's own service terms and quotes remain that business's responsibility.

Delivery, revisions and support

Work begins after payment has been verified and we have reviewed the required details, access and approved scope. We confirm the estimated delivery date with project kickoff. Additional websites, custom workflows, and out-of-scope revisions require separate written approval. Future support, renewals and changes are separately agreed.

AI, privacy and account access

Customers must provide accurate information and have authorization to connect channels and process their end customers' data. AI assistants can make mistakes; they must not promise final prices, confirm unverified bookings, or make sensitive decisions without appropriate configuration and review. We use secure collaborator permissions or authorized connections and never require emailed passwords.

Payments, cancellations and rights

Secure payments are handled by external processors. Please consult our refund policy; applicable statutory rights remain unaffected. A cancellation request is not completed until confirmed. We do not guarantee leads, revenue, SEO positions or business outcomes.

Earlier agreements

Orders and agreements accepted before the change remain governed by their originally accepted terms. Former offerings are archived rather than actively marketed; historical payments and obligations are not erased.
