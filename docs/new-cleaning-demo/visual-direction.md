# New Cleaning Booking Demo — Visual Direction

Status: design exploration only; isolated branch, not production.

## Goal
A completely new, premium cleaning-company website experience that visitors can interact with. The Launch Era's software offer appears only after the cleaning-company experience.

## Sections and image plan
1. Hero: real-looking professional cleaner working in a luminous living room; navy edge gradient and rounded corners.
2. Booking: interactive compact 3-step card directly under hero; service, property/address, contact/review. No background photo needed beyond subtle home interior accent.
3. Services: four separate photos showing standard residential cleaning, deep cleaning, move-in/out, and commercial cleaning.
4. Why Choose Us: cleaner wiping a kitchen or bathroom, natural light.
5. Before/After: genuine matched pairs only when sourced/approved; never pass unrelated generated scenes off as the same job.
6. How It Works: icons and small photography details; not a fake dashboard.
7. Reviews: use verified reviews only; otherwise label content as sample.
8. Trusted By / Areas: real cleaning contexts; no invented partner logos.
9. Extras: pet-friendly care, custom cleaning, recurring service, cleaning supplies.
10. Final section: The Launch Era CTA offering this booking experience to cleaning businesses.

## Visual system
Warm ivory, sky blue, navy, yellow CTA, soft black. Editorial serif headlines, limited handwritten blue accents. Rounded cards, ample breathing room, polished mobile layout, accessible contrast and reduced-motion support.

## Implementation safeguards
- Do not modify production/main without explicit approval.
- Do not reuse the failed AppDeploy source as a production base.
- Keep this demo isolated and test all 3 booking steps, mobile layout, navigation, and CTA actions.
- No actual booking submission, payment, email, or backend integration until explicitly configured and tested.
- The generated composite concept images are visual references, not final independent production photo assets.
