# THE LAUNCH ERA — PUBLIC BRAND SYSTEM / VERSION 1
**Owner standard • 2026-11-06 • For all public websites, demo concepts, free resources, class, and customer emails**

## Single parent identity
All public products belong to **The Launch Era**, with a consistent visual standard:
- Warm Ivory `#FAF8F3`, Ink `#191919`, Butter Yellow `#F2D85B`, Soft Blue `#BED8E9`.
- Corporate typography: Prompt + Futura where locally available/licensed; accessible web-safe sans fallback. Optional elegant editorial serif for feature headlines, paired with a restrained sans face.
- Editorial hierarchy; one true primary CTA per offer; content clarity over decoration; meaningful image crops; consistent contrast and generous whitespace.
- No generic Canva-looking templates, excessive gradients, fake reviews, imaginary testimonials, fabricated prices, overstuffed navigation, or vague AI claims.
- Mobile-first starting at 375px, also verify 320/390/430, 768, 1440. Keep touch targets 44px+, visible focus, reduced motion, layout without horizontal overflow.
- Display real product screens / brand photographs and realistic demonstrations; never imply a mockup is the functioning app. Avoid reusing the same food/product photo under different product names.

## IMPORTANT: Two levels of branding
1. **Corporate products / funnels** (The Launch Era main site, Website for Your Business, classes, Content Lab, podcast, freebies): same TLE palette, high-quality offer card, typography system, spacing, email formatting, form safety, accessible CTA, privacy/legal links and footer. Keep each product's brand personality and copy.
2. **Client/demo websites** (Wok Social, Crumb, Yummm, SOVA, Golden Grid, Casa Brownie, etc.): the demo's own typography, palette and photographic direction **must remain distinct**. Only shared standards are technical UX, disclosure of concept demo where appropriate, and a discreet, linked `Powered by The Launch Era` studio signature. **Never recolor every demo to ivory/yellow.** Do not introduce a live ordering/payment illusion into fictional demos.

## Public email system (Resend)
- Consistent 560–600px HTML email table with Ivory canvas, discreet Butter Yellow top line, Georgia editorial headline, Ink text, Soft Blue secondary instructional box, **one black CTA**, clear reply-to/support, legal footer and unsub link only for marketing consent campaigns.
- Different types, never conflate: **marketing introduction → lead confirmation → class/freebie update → purchase confirmation → product access/welcome → support/transactional → podcast episode**.
- Use an actual sender verified by the owner; never invent delivery or payment verification. Do not alter active aliases/automation triggers until mapped and tested.
- Test all current public product email families with `[TEST]` in subject and prominent test banner. Send only to explicitly approved test address and sender; no bulk audiences, no signup, no purchases, no fake account access, no real unsubscribe URL tokens in samples.
- Keep Spanish and English messages individually, no mixed-language wall of text. Marketing for The Launch Era in **English by default**, except explicitly bilingual products or client choice; private podcast can be Spanish.
- Design templates in draft first and verify metadata; publishing a template does not automatically wire it to a workflow.
- Avoid legacy/archived cleaning-app subscription emails in new campaigns.

## Functional QA / completion proof
- GitHub commit and build; published Pages run **success**; externally load exact URL (including asset paths and image HTTP status) before saying live.
- Real viewport screenshots and interaction tests; inspect what appears, not only what the build reports.
- Test email deliverability to one approved address and inspect actual content in an email client; a Resend template draft alone is NOT a sent or active automation.
- Preserve source folders and previously approved design. Do not duplicate projects; check `main` before edits; do not overwrite other agents' work.
- Record paths/commit IDs, source/preview links, actual test results and limitations.

**Scope note**: legacy site projects, prior products and old sent emails remain historical evidence and must not be republished or deleted as part of design harmonization.
